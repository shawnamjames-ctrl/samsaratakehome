EVIDENCE REVIEW

What a theme must pass before it becomes a finding

Every published theme is tested through the same five checks. Confidence summarizes how consistently it survives those checks; it does not describe severity.

  1. 01
    Check the denominator

    Calculate each rate against the correct Fleet or Driver review population, not the combined total.

  2. 02
    Compare separate time periods

    Compare recent reviews with an earlier, separate period. No review appears on both sides, so a temporary spike is easier to distinguish from a persistent pattern.

  3. 03
    Compare platforms fairly

    Check both the share of all reviews and the share among reviews that already report a problem. This separates a more negative review population from a genuinely different problem mix.

  4. 04
    Challenge the pattern

    Review counterevidence, alternative explanations, severity, versions, and sensitivity to one additional review.

  5. 05
    Set the decision boundary

    A human reviewer approves the finding and states what public reviews support, as well as what still requires internal data.
