# Samsara Mobile Product Intelligence

Hey there! Thanks for taking a look at this take home. I'm excited to share more of this
project with you this week. This is an evidence-backed Product Operations case study
based on 927 US public written reviews of Samsara Driver and Samsara Fleet. The main
deliverable is in the form of an HTML with the CPO Memo and Methodology links embedded
and below. More details on how to navigate the repo below:

- **[Open the interactive project](https://shawnamjames-ctrl.github.io/samsaratakehome/)**

- [Read the methodology note](deliverables/Samsara_Methodology_Note.pdf)

## What this work answers

- Which recurring customer themes matter most
- What product actions those themes suggest
- What additional research is needed before committing roadmap investment
- How new public-review signals can be monitored without overstating what they prove

## Project folders

- [`deliverables/`](deliverables/) contains the final CPO memo and methodology note.
- [`technical-appendix/`](technical-appendix/) contains the optional evidence, public
  data, analytical definitions, site materials, and monitoring system for a deeper review.

## Scope and interpretation

The approved analysis covers 927 US public written reviews collected across the Apple
App Store and Google Play: 800 for Samsara Driver and 127 for Samsara Fleet. Public
reviews help prioritize investigation; they do not establish all-customer prevalence,
root cause, or release causality.
